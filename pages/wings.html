Buffalo Wings

Description

Classic recipe for chicken wings with 2 different types of sauces.

Ingredients

Wings

  • 1/2 pound of chicken wings
  • 1 tbsp of cajun seasoning

Classic Buffalo Sauce

  • 1/3 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup hot sauce (preferably Frank's Red Hot)
  • 1/8 teaspoon of xanthum gum (optional)

Honey Siracha Sauce

  • 1/2 cup Siracha sauce
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1.5 teaspoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon garilc powder
  • salt to taste
    • <2 tablespoons butter>

Steps

Wings

  1. Cut wings into segments.
  2. Pat wings dry.
  3. Preheat airfryer to 380 degrees. While preheating, season wings liberally with cajun seasoning.
  4. Cook wings at 380 for 24 minutes, then increase heat to 400 and cook for 6 minutes.
  5. Toss wings immediately in sauce after cooking.

Buffalo Sauce

  1. Melt butter in small saucepan on low heat.
  2. Add hot sauce, stir until incorporated
  3. Let sauce simmer on low for 3-4 minutes, stir in xanthum gum if desired.

Honey Siracha sauce

  1. Whisk together the sriracha, honey, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and salt to taste in a small pot over medium-low heat.
  2. Simmer for 3-4 minutes to let the flavors combine.
  3. Gently whisk in the butter until it melts through and the sauce thickens slightly.
